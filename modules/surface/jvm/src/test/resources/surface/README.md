# Surface Test Resources

These files are tiny synthetic fixtures for ScalaFIM surface IO tests. They are
not copied from FreeSurfer, neurosurf, or TemplateFlow assets.

- `mini_lh_smoothwm.asc`: FreeSurfer/SUMA-style ASCII surface with 4 vertices
  and 2 zero-based faces.
- `tetra_lh_midthickness.surf.gii`: ASCII GIFTI surface with 4 vertices,
  2 zero-based triangle faces, and a POINTSET CoordinateSystemTransformMatrix.
