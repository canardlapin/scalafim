## Accepted limits

- `mvpaFoundationAdmission` and `IdentityPrototype` remain unpublished,
  test-only courts. The prototype is bounded to 64 coordinates, uses tiny
  dense restrictions, and records caller-declared rather than content-verified
  metadata. M1 must replace it rather than preserve a second axis hierarchy.
- Row views and the synthetic timing receipt do not qualify whole-brain
  streaming, allocations, or the frozen JDK 21/Node 22 resource profile. The
  local engineering run used Java 25.0.1 and Node 26.7.0.
- The independent migration and searchlight fixtures establish their declared
  bounded parity only. They do not establish complete PyMVPA equivalence,
  classifier superiority, or a product performance budget.
- Thresholded SIMPLS remains unavailable until ScalaFIM has its own adapter and
  JVM/Scala.js consumer court.
- Classification performance, Haufe interpretation, localization, rank
  determination, inference, calibration, group analysis, and scientific
  superiority remain assigned to later milestone gates.

These are scope boundaries, not hidden M0 failures. The accepted next step is
M1.01: move the proven identity laws into one production evidence layer over
the admitted provider graph, then retire the prototype as its laws migrate.
