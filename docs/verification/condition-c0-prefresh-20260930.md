## Recorded historical use

The earlier independent audit and findings are preserved in this evidence
directory. They account for the original 33 C0 raw/meta pairs, including the
opaque smoke, failed controls, and source-bound development executions. They
found no prospective root in recorded C0 commands or parseable results. The
prior Mote decision is the basis for retiring `7000930101` despite its absence
from emitted seed traces.

The new audit extends that record through the snapshot timestamp in
`stream-history-audit.json`. It scans all currently listed execution-root and
Gaussian-repair raw logs, their available metadata, and repository-specific
Codex sessions on 2026-09-29/30. Raw records and tool-code mentions are treated
separately: a prospective constructor in source or a seed in a task packet is
not evidence of numerical execution. Command literals are indexed by session,
line, and hash for independent inspection. The scanner supports both custom
and ordinary tool-call record formats.

The supplementary session selection requires a matching repository or task cwd
prefix in the first 100 lines. The command index reconstructs literal `cmd`
strings. Older or other-cwd sessions, dynamically constructed commands, and
wrapper-only launches can escape that supplementary scan. All 286 observed
candidate records in this snapshot use the custom tool-call/output format.

The audited snapshot contains 617 raw logs and 50 C0/repair metadata command
records. It finds zero prospective seed mentions in those raw logs, zero emitted
fresh-study records, and zero recorded metadata invocations of `study fresh`.
The 68 indexed prospective/fresh-related shell literals describe source creation,
inspection, tracking, or coordination rather than a fresh numerical launch.
The 289 selected repository sessions contain 286 candidate tool records; those
include outputs and declarations and are not counted as executed fresh work.

Two unparseable existing metadata files are explicitly listed in the audit.
They are unrelated bootstrap/calibration jobs. Absent sidecars are outside that
