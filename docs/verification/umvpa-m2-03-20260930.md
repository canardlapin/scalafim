# UMVPA M2.03 query implementation note

`relation/Queries.scala` introduces separate first-order and second-order query
types over the admitted `Relation` core.

- `FirstOrderQuery[E, C]` accepts an identified `Table[E, C]` contrast map and
  returns a `FirstOrderPattern[C, N]`; it cannot produce a second-order form.
- `RelationPair[EL, NL, ER, NR]` preserves ordered and potentially rectangular
  endpoint spaces. `SecondOrderQuery` accepts only `Table[EL, ER]` experimental
  and `Table[NL, NR]` neural closures.
- Effect and neural closures produce separate signed, rectangular forms.
  Neither form receives a symmetry, PSD, distance, or zeroing claim.
- `scalar` evaluates `tr(H^T B_L K B_R^T)` one right-effect basis column at a time through admitted Multivar maps. It requires declared owned replay for both endpoints. Effect and neural forms remain lazy, preserve endpoint origins, and evaluate forward/adjoint closures without full operator materialization. Open
  transports retain the pair without evaluating either relation and never form
  a Kronecker product.

The implementation handoff intentionally leaves numerical closure-oracle and
JVM/JS qualification results to the serial verifier and `QueryOracleSuite`
owner. The intended independent checks are direct dense contraction,
forward/adjoint equivalence, and pair-reversal transpose laws on asymmetric,
rectangular fixtures.

Pair reversal is distinct from the Frobenius adjoint of open transport. The latter maps an experimental closure H to B_L^T H B_R via `closeExperimental`; it is checked by the independent contraction identity. Relation replay declarations and operator capabilities are caller/provider contracts, not new ownership certificates.
