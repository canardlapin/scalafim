# UMVPA M3.10 operational work units

LocalExecution uses LocalPipelineRunner for finite in-memory work units. A
WorkAddress includes PlanId, split, replicate, stage, MeasurementId and a canonical
measurement coordinate. Six Seed.derive stream domains encode coordinates plus
the full hexadecimal plan identity and measurement ID. Neither worker assignment
nor traversal order chooses randomness. This does not claim collision-free PRNGs.
Measurement ID/coordinate mappings and expected addresses are validated before
execution. Attempts retain their supplied order; reduction/OOF output is canonical.

Acquire, compute, close and BeforeCommit faults retain their typed causes. Compute
and close faults are combined. A close failure or conflicting committed retry is
terminal and cannot be erased by a subsequent success or cancellation. Recoverable
compute/precommit attempts may be retried. Exact equal immutable contributions
commit once; the fingerprint alone cannot conceal changed reducer or OOF payloads.
Generic equality is not deep numerical equality; programs must supply immutable
contributions. Canonical reduction exceptions become ReductionFailed with committed
coverage retained, including failure of empty on an empty family.

Complete requires every expected address committed. Missing attempts, explicit
partial results, cancellation and failure retain coverage and committed subsets.
Cancellation cannot erase known failures. In-memory execution explicitly refuses
durable checkpoint/resume; no fresh-process recovery or distributed runtime is
claimed.

Regression tests cover schedule/seed stability, missing units, retries with changed
payloads and unchanged fingerprints, precommit failure, partial/cancelled results,
compute plus close faults, terminal close failures before/after a commit, throwing
control/acquisition/precommit callbacks and reduction faults.

Owning gates are separate mvpaJVM/test and mvpaJS/test invocations. Exact source,
commands/results and independent review are in the integration report.
