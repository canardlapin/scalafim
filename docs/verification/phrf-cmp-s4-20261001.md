## Theory-grounded checks

- ridge to 0 recovers LSS (pooled grouping; 1e-8 at ridge `1e-9 mean q`); the condition-grouped form at 0 differs from it;
- ridge to infinity gives the condition-mean fit (1e-9 at `1e14 mean q`); deviation norm strictly decreasing in the ridge;
- edf is 1 at no penalty, strictly decreasing, tends to 0; equal information gives `p = q (1/edf - 1)`;
- on a disjoint-support design `q_i = d_i^2 (1 - d_i^2 / S_g)` exactly, which gives the `1 - 1/n_c` centring factor;
- unit change: rLSS on `x^R = kappa x^P` with `p = kappa^2 (1 - 1/n_c) lambda` equals the PHRF-unit single-deviation solve, `a_P = kappa a_R` (1e-10);
- the centring factor equals `e_i' (lambda (I - M)) e_i / lambda` of the `TrialBanded` penalty structure (`lambda (delta_ij - same(i,j)/n_c)`), and `kappa^2` is read from the preparation's own packed Gram blocks;
- endpoint behaviour: no deviations select grid indices 0 to 1 (strong shrinkage), strong persistent deviations select index 8 (4 seeds each; `S4-ENDPOINTS`);
- poisoned held-out run: fold df map unchanged when only the held-out run's data and regressors are altered; its score does change;
- folds: one per run, each leaving exactly that run out, training and test rows partition the time axis;
- sample times: the prepared trial regressors equal the canonical column evaluated on `FitInputs.sampleTime` (`k * TR` from 0) and whitened; the PHRF-side `SamplingFrame` in the tests sets `startTime = 0` (default is `TR/2`) and a test asserts it equals the generator's sample times.

## Ambiguities resolved

1. **The mapping is the identity on the penalty scale when both sides share q.** Design 2.2 defines `edf(p)` and `edf(r)` with the same
   `q_i`, so `edf(r_k) = edf(p_k)` gives `r_k = p_k`. The bisection is implemented and tested for the general case (different native and ridge
   information; closed form `r = c p` when `q_R = c q_N`), and `DfMapping.map(grid, scale, qRidge, qNative)` takes both. In the pilot call both are the rLSS `q`.
   The substantive content is therefore the scale factor `PenaltyScale`.
2. **Scale factor** `p = kappa^2 (1 - 1/n_c) lambda`: `kappa^2` is the ratio of whitened squared regressor norms (rLSS canonical over PHRF shape at
   the canonical coefficients); `(1 - 1/n_c)` comes from rLSS moving one trial against its condition under PHRF's `lambda (I - M)`
   penalty. The caller (S5) supplies the PHRF shape coefficients; S4 supplies `PenaltyScale.phrfNorm2(prep, coefficients)`.
3. **Fold score** (design left it as "predicted R2 in whitened space"): `1 - RSS/TSS` pooled over held-out rows and voxels, after removing the
   held-out run's own nuisance by projection from both data and prediction (its baseline columns have no training support). Frozen here.
4. **Nuisance columns of the held-out run** have no training support and are dropped from the training fit (exactly-zero columns only).
5. **Trial regressors** are the leading `nEvents` columns of the whitened S2 pre-fit design (layout checked), so they are the same peak-1 `Spmg1`
   columns the AR fit used; native amplitudes are therefore already E-trial (conversion factor 1).
6. **LSA** (`Ols` on whitened arrays) and the design's "GLS": on arrays whitened by the shared plan they coincide.

## Revision 2 (2026-10-02): rLSS matched to PHRF on true effective df (owner decision)

The first revision (332863ba) mapped on a q-based surrogate, which made `r_k = p_k` by construction and the 5 % end flag unable
to fire. Revision 2 replaces it. Everything above that concerns the surrogate edf, the `kappa^2 (1 - 1/n_c)` mapping and the
"identity" ambiguity note is superseded by this section.

### Definition (committed first, 3066dfaf, for S5)

`edf = tr(S X) = sum_i d ahat_i / d a_i`: the trace of the trial-amplitude smoother (`ahat = S y`), condition means INCLUDED,
nuisance projected out. rLSS: `sum_i [phi_i + (1 - phi_i) q_i/(q_i + r)]` (`RidgeLssPrepared.amplitudeEdf`; `N` at `r = 0`, `G`
conditions as `r -> infinity`). PHRF at a fixed shape: `tr(W^-1 X~'X~)`, `W = X~'X~ + lambda (I - M)`. Full text: scaladoc on
`object DfMapping` and design section 2.2 (dated note).

### What changed

- `RidgeLssPrepared.amplitudeEdf`, `selfLoading`; the q-based quantity is renamed `informationRatio` / `InformationRatio` (diagnostic).
- `DfMapping.map(grid, targetEdf, curve, diagnostic)` with `curve = EdfCurve.rlss(prepared)` (a typed refusal when some trial has `q_i <= eps`, since `edf(0)` would not be `N`): bisection on the rLSS amplitude edf; targets above `N` saturate
  at ridge 0, below `G` at a large cap; the 5 % end flag compares achieved and target edf at both grid ends. It is a GUARD: on the pilot path `edf_P` lies in `(G, N]` and `edf_R` covers `(G, N]` by construction, so it fires only for fabricated or out-of-contract targets (the tests fabricate them); it cannot fire on a well-formed pilot dataset.
- `PenaltyScale` is a reported diagnostic (`DfMap.scaleDiagnosticRidges`), never the mapping; `PenaltyScale.Identity` is `private[phrfcmp]`.
- `RlssSettings(grid, scale, targets: Option[EdfTargets])`; `EdfTargets.forRuns(runs)` is PHRF's edf for the design of exactly those
  runs (training runs of a fold, all runs for the final fit), computed from the design alone. Without targets the rLSS arm refuses
  (`native_no_edf_targets`).
- F3: `FoldScore.predictedSignal(regressors, amplitudes)` is shared (held-out rows x held-out trials times trials x voxels). Voxel pooling
  rule, documented in the scaladoc: ALL delivered scored voxels of the held-out block, regardless of decode status.
- F5: runner-level test that the fold scores and selection are unchanged by a response with an enormous in-span baseline (800 x each
  nuisance column). Declared: the held-out baseline is estimated IN-SAMPLE by projecting the held-out run's own nuisance out of data and
  prediction; this is symmetric across arms (PHRF uses the same `FoldScore`).
- F6: the penalty-scale diagnostic is recomputed on each training fold's `n_c` (and on the final design's); `q`, the rLSS edf curve and the
  edf targets are recomputed per training-fold design. F7: the `qRidge`/`qNative` mapping no longer exists, so its identity short-circuit is moot.
- Test fixture `JointEdf` (test sources): a dense PHRF-style joint edf computed from a design alone, standing in for S5's targets in tests.
  S5's committed `PhrfTrialTheorySuite` and `PhrfTrialRealSuite` called the removed `nativePenalties`/no-target path; they were updated
  minimally (supply `JointEdf` targets, assert the diagnostic ridges) in this commit.
