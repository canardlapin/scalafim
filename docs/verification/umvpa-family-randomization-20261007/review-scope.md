## Numerical argument checked

With fixed targets T and nuisance Z, the declared error law is separable joint
Gaussian with known positive-definite row shape. W whitens that shape and the
provider complement Q satisfies `Q'Q=I` and `Q'WZ=0`. The actual model is

`X* = Q'WX = (Q'WT) B + E*`.

For a true-null subset of voxels J, every column of `B_J` is zero. Thus `X*_J`
has a common spherical row law with arbitrary joint voxel covariance and is
invariant under the same row permutation. Fitting one voxel never uses another
voxel's response; nonnull coefficients cannot enter null-voxel statistics.
Every whole-family maximum is at least the maximum over the true-null subset
for that same action. This is the candidate null-subset argument for strong
single-step max-statistic control, subject to the declared law, provider action
and subsequent frozen numerical/rate qualification.

The response fit is the complete fixed target design, with residual df equal
to residual-coordinate rows minus target rank. Its omnibus numerator is the
fitted-response squared norm divided by target rank; its denominator is the
full-model estimated residual variance. Existing independent OLS and known-row
GLS loading fixtures test the numerical estimand and coefficients. A separate
tiny scalar-regression permutation oracle uses the exact augmented working
nuisance basis and checks orthonormality/orthogonality before comparing maxima.
Its finite-group numerical agreement does not certify all mathematical ties.
