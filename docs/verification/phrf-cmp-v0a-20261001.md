## TX distribution: review notes

- Undershoot ratio of accepted shapes: 49% are below 0.05 and 31% below 0.01 (median 0.053, 10th percentile 0). The box leans Gaussian-like.
- Stratified medians by undershoot ratio:

| Undershoot ratio | Gaussian median | Cascade34 median |
| --- | --- | --- |
| < 0.01 | 0.112 | 0.300 |
| 0.01 to 0.1 | 0.104 | 0.371 |
| >= 0.1 | 0.210 | 0.454 |

- Consequence: because the Gaussian-like members are the hardest for the Gaussian chart, the 0.05 gate on the pooled median is conservative. It matters for tier-2 (C-SUP) interpretation: TX cells are partly near-Gaussian, so the gate does not show that TX is far from PHRF's families at every undershoot level.
- IL is not uniformly smooth rise-fall-recovery. 189 of 1024 shapes show small wiggles; the worst has a -1.8% dip at 0.5 s and a +0.15% overshoot at 21 s (relative to peak). Read the kernels as "IL shapes with a negligible causal-truncation artifact and occasional small ripples", not as strictly smooth.

## TX parametrization frozen here (for manifest v0)

`h(t) = L((t-T1)/D1) - (1+c) L((t-T2)/D2) + c L((t-T3)/D3)`, `L(x) = 1/(1+e^-x)`, `T2 = T1+g2`, `T3 = T2+g3`, t >= 0 else 0. Form from Lindquist et al. 2009 NeuroImage 45:S187 eq. A10 (read). Box: T1 [2.5,5], D1 [0.5,1.2], g2 [2.5,8], D2 [0.8,2.5], g3 [1.5,7], D3 [1,3], c [0,0.5]. Acceptance by rejection on attained peak 4-8 s, FWHM 3-7 s, undershoot ratio 0-0.4, plus |h(0)| <= 0.02 peak and |h(48)| <= 0.01 peak (causal truncation negligible, baseline reached). Sum-to-zero alpha constraint (a1 = 1, a2 = -(1+c), a3 = c) is this harness's reading of "return to baseline".

## Protocol choices, assumptions, limitations

- Structured trial noise: rank 3, 30 % of marginal noise variance (LOWRANK_FRAC), temporal components AR with the same coefficients; fraction is not in the protocol.
- Trial ISI is read as onset-to-onset; runs are rejection-sampled so the last onset is >= 12 s before run end. Stimulus effects and repeat noise are drawn independently per voxel. Heavy-tailed draws are t(3) scaled to unit SD; i.i.d. cell uses total SD sqrt(0.5^2 + 0.25^2) of the mean absolute condition peak.
- Condition amplitude convention (explicit): the amplitude of condition c is the peak absolute height of the superposed condition regressor (the sum over all of that condition's events of the unit-peak kernel, sampled at the TR grid, with overlaps adding), not the height of a single-event response; trial: single-trial peak. The SNR target for trial cells applies to the condition-mean signal (`signal_condition_mean`).
- T-G cells (T-TX-fast, T-TX-jit, T-TX-slow; scored by GLMsingle) use TR-aligned nominal onsets, by the per-cell flag `tr_aligned_onsets` (recorded in the manifest cell spec; trial cells only). Gaps and first onset are integer multiples of TR within the ISI bounds. Latency jitter in T-TX-jit still perturbs the truth onsets (`ev_onset_true`) by up to 1 s. All other cells keep sub-TR onsets on the 0.1 s grid.
- Sparse condition cell: 300 events at ISI 10-14 s do not fit 600 s, so as many as fit are placed. TR 2 cell keeps 600 s (300 samples). AR(2) coefficients and the 2 s neural duration are harness choices.
- Nuisance coefficients are N(0,1) and drawn from the noise stream.
- Reproducibility of bytes is conditional on numpy 2.2.6 / scipy 1.15.3 (recorded in manifests; `requirements.lock`).
- Unverified: IL sum-to-zero constraint against the 2007 HBM paper text (2009 eq. A10 verified); the scalafim cross-check; Scala reading of the npz; the GLMsingle v0 gate (other agent).
