- `Aggregator.scala`: `PilotAggregator.aggregate` / `aggregateGuarded` (never throws, class name only),
  `SealedDiagnostics` (binary blob), `RunComplete.line` (total CPU hours only).
- `SafeMessage.scala`: messages are a constructor token plus named integer totals; no `Double` entry.

## Resolved ambiguities and limits

1. Imputation and Fisher z are voxel-level (statuses are per voxel): the voxel's z is the mean over conditions; the
   imputed z is the lowest voxel-level z among the pool arms.
2. The T-G pairs (PHRF vs GLMs-D) use the pool T-TX arms plus GLMs-D; the other T-TX pairs use the four T-TX arms. C-TG-.5
   uses the condition pool.
3. Location invariance holds for dataset-level scores (tested on `Centred`, and end to end for a multiplicative offset
   of a comparator's errors when no voxel is refused). With refusals, a shift of one comparator's voxel scores can
   change which arm is the family's worst and so changes other arms' imputed values; this coupling is the imputation
   channel the design names, and it is why imputation runs sealed-side.
4. ICC clusters may be unbalanced (refused voxels); k0 replaces k. Undefined ICC (fewer than two clusters, no within df,
   MSB = MSW = 0) renders as `"degenerate"`, the same token as the coverage rule.
5. Pooled rates emit rate and the attempted denominator; the numerator is derivable only through the rate. Counts of
   imputed, excluded, floored and zero-variance voxels, and complete-case sigma, are sealed.
6. Realized df per pair is the number of datasets with a defined endpoint minus 1; df < 14 refuses the aggregation
   (`DfTooSmall`), and a corpus with D < 15 refuses (`TooFewDatasets`).
7. The sealed blob is not byte-stable under a location change (complete-case sigma differs at the last bit); only the
   whitelist is asserted byte-identical.

## Follow-ups after independent review (APPROVE-WITH-NITS of 83caf4e2)

1. **Owner decision (2026-10-02):** trial imputation at a refused voxel is the voxel-level minimum, the lowest
   condition-averaged z among the family's gating arms. Recorded as a dated line in design section 3.2.
2. Trial imputation direction: `FollowUpSuite` builds a corpus with exact per-arm Fisher z (Gram-Schmidt
   construction), a refused PHRF voxel in even datasets, and checks sigma and the delta-z ordering against closed
   forms (T-TX/T-TS pairs and the T-G pair). Mutation A (`Worse.Lower` to `Higher` in `trialPair`): 1 failure.
3. Pooled rates now exercised with `Failed` voxels (FIR in condition cells, GLMs-D in T-TX). Mutation K
   (`isMissing` to `== Refused`): 1 failure.
4. Pool membership: a GLMs-D arm (different z, or refused everywhere) leaves every non-GLMs T-TX/T-TS sigma
   byte-equal and changes the T-G pair; `conditionPair` and `trialPair` ignore extra arms in `ds.arms`.
5. Planted scan extended to a planted trial delta z (0.123456 and an independently computed mean): trial sigma equals the
   closed form and is unchanged between planted values to 1e-9 (the trial path goes through Pearson r, so it is not
   byte-exact); text, error messages, `toString`s, forced exceptions through `PilotAggregator.guard` (class name only; message,
   cause and trace dropped) and sealed bytes carry neither value.
6. `UnequalDatasetCounts`, `TooFewDatasets`, `DfTooSmall`, `TooFewValues` messages are the constructor token only (no
   digits, tested); counts stay in fields; `ScoreError.toString` is the message.
7. Constant true amplitudes: `SigmaEntry` now holds `SigmaValue` (`Estimate(sigma, df)` or `Degenerate`). A cell whose truth
   is constant within a condition in any dataset renders its pairs as `"degenerate"` for sigma and df; the rest of the
   output is unchanged. Protocol v3.2 section 6, R3 trial endpoint, undefined cases: "If the true within-condition
   variance is 0 (`alpha_true = 0`), the cell reports E-dev MSE only."
8. Test and fixture labels renamed to "anova(lm()) ICC" (psych values kept in a comment); df = 14 accepted and df = 13
   refused are explicit tests (15 or 14 defined endpoints of 20).
9. README section for S8 added to `modules/phrf-comparison/README.md` (S4's section untouched).
